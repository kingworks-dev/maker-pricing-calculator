# Free Maker Pricing Calculator (Etsy and craft-market sellers)

Price handmade goods so they actually pay you. Enter materials, labor hours, your hourly rate and packaging to get a retail price, profit per item, margin, wholesale price and monthly profit. No signup, runs in your browser.

**Use it free:** https://kingworks-dev.github.io/maker-pricing-calculator/

## What it calculates

- Cost to make one item (materials + labor + packaging and fees)
- Recommended retail price from your target markup
- Profit per item and margin
- Wholesale price (half of retail) and profit at wholesale
- Monthly profit at your sales volume
